# Google Play listing: WordHunt Offline v0.2.0 (`com.offerpk.wordhunt`)

**App name (30):** WordHunt Offline: Word Search
**Short description (80):** Find hidden words on a letter grid. Daily puzzle, themes, fully offline.

**Full description**

WordHunt Offline is a calm word search you can play anywhere, no internet or account needed.

Drag across the letters to find every hidden word. Words run across, down, diagonally and backwards. Pick a theme (Animals, Food, Travel or All) or play the Daily puzzle, the same grid for everyone each day, and build your streak.

★ Endless puzzles in 4 themes, plus a new Daily puzzle every day
★ Daily reward calendar (7 days) and a Daily challenge
★ Skin Shop: tile styles and backdrops. Tap to preview, or try any skin free for 24 hours
★ Achievements and a Stats page
★ A short tutorial on your first puzzle and a How to play card
★ Hints reveal one letter when you're stuck
★ Accessibility: reduce motion, larger text, high contrast
★ Fully offline, tiny download

Fair play: coins are cosmetic only. They're earned by playing and can never be bought or cashed out. Ads are optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (for a hint or a free skin trial), plus an occasional ad between puzzles after your first session. Never on launch.
